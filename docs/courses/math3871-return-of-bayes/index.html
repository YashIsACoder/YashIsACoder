Return of Bayes

Hope you have a lot of paper

YMYashwanth MadanComputer science and statistics, UNSW Sydney
Difficulty 9/10Workload 7/10Enjoyment 10/10Take it again?YesTaken25T3

Course about bayesian inference

The good#

Most interesting topic in statistics. You break away from the primitive and boring frequentist statistics which is forced upon you since high school and actually play with the theory which provides more intuitive results.

A lot of people's intuition on what confidence intervals are, is actually what credible intervals are.

The assignment was really fun and actually using software to learn bayesian inference is honestly the best way to learn it -- moreso than the annoying integrals to setup which you are forced to do by hand.

The hard parts#

The amount of writing you need to do to just solve menial problems is actually insane.

You get a first hand experience onto why this field was basically dead for most of its original inception until computers came along.

How I'd approach it#

Start from software even though the course starts with theorems and writing. This field feels so intuitive and its like learning newtonian physics, make sure you get a good grasp of all the concepts so writing solutions is a matter of when it'll stop rather than how to do it.

The exams are killer, make sure you're not overfitting on the workshop solutions. I honestly really enjoyed the exam regardless of how hard it was, it favoured your reasoning and pattern recognition over pure grinding. I did well but I wouldn't say it was good for my confidence though haha

Who should take it#

Any statistics student worth their salt should have a go at it. Its the pinnacle of the degree and whilst it is a grind, it opens up the best part statistics has to offer that your usual applied/pure friends won't ever wrestle with.
